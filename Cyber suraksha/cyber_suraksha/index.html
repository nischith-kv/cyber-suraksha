Threat Intelligence

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails or links pretending to be banks or officials to steal credentials.

Tip: Always verify the sender's email address and URL.

OTP Fraud

Scammers trick you into sharing your One-Time Password to authorize transactions.

Tip: Never share your OTP with anyone over a call.

Impersonation Calls

Fake police, customs, or courier calls demanding money to clear parcels or cases.

Tip: Disconnect and call the official department directly.
